ℹ️ What is an Emission Factor?

The emission factor is the amount of CO₂ equivalent (kg) emitted per kg of food wasted. These values are based on IPCC / FAO life-cycle assessments covering farming, transport, processing, and decomposition. Higher values = more carbon-intensive to produce.
